Your data. What GearWatch keeps, and who can see it. What’s stored
Your email, username, profile picture, banner and title. Your wishlist, collection, outfits, measurements, budget, notes, photos and stories.
Who sees what
Everyone signed inYour username, profile picture and title.
Friends you’ve acceptedYour wishlist, collection, outfits, what you paid, and your stories. Stories can be limited to close friends.
Only youYour email, measurements, budget, private notes, and anything you hide from friends.
Anyone with a share linkOnly what’s in that link: one item or outfit, or an invite with your username, profile picture, title and how many items and outfits you have. Links show a preview card in chats.
Photos
Photos are private. Only you and your friends can open them. A share link carries its own photo links, which work for a year, and a preview card that anyone with the link can see.
What GearWatch doesn’t do
No ads and no tracking. Nothing is sold or passed on.
Where it lives
Accounts, lists and photos are stored with Supabase. When you check a price or fill in from a link, that shop link goes through GearWatch’s price helper on Cloudflare. Nothing about you is sent with it.
Deleting
Settings › Delete account removes your account, lists, photos, stories and friends for good.
